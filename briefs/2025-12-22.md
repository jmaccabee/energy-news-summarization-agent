Dec 22 \x2725 Brief

1. **Renewables poised for rapid growth in Asia-Pacific**: A new report by the International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA) predicts that the Asia-Pacific region is set to experience rapid growth in the renewables sector. The report highlights favorable market conditions, government policies, and increased investments that will drive the expansion of renewable energy capacity in the region.

2. **Biden administration releases new climate finance plan**: The Biden administration has unveiled a new plan to support climate finance initiatives both domestically and globally. The plan includes efforts to increase investments in sustainable infrastructure, renewable energy projects, and climate resilience measures. The administration aims to advance its climate agenda through financial support and partnerships with the private sector.

3. **California regulators approve new rules for electrification of buildings**: The California Public Utilities Commission has approved new rules aimed at accelerating the electrification of buildings in the state. The rules require utilities to develop programs to support the transition to electric space and water heating, with a focus on reducing greenhouse gas emissions from buildings.

4. **Norwegian energy major Equinor to invest in US offshore wind project**: Equinor has announced plans to invest in the Beacon Wind offshore wind project off the coast of Massachusetts. The project is expected to have a capacity of up to 2.4 gigawatts and will contribute to Equinor's goal of becoming a global leader in offshore wind development.

5. **UK government announces new funding for green hydrogen projects**: The UK government has unveiled £105 million in new funding to support the development of green hydrogen projects. The funding will be used to build new hydrogen production facilities, upgrade existing infrastructure, and support research and development efforts in the sector.
